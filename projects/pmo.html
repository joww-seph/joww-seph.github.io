PMO — Power Monitor Operator

AI-powered residential energy monitoring and protection system built on ESP32. Real-time electrical tracking, automated safety control, and intelligent energy insights with both offline and cloud capabilities.

Live Demo → View on GitHub →

Problem

Philippine households lack real-time visibility into their electrical consumption, relying only on monthly bills that provide no actionable insights. Electrical risks such as overcurrent, voltage fluctuations, and poor power factor often go undetected, posing safety hazards. Existing smart monitors are heavily cloud-dependent, becoming unusable during internet outages. Additionally, users cannot identify which appliances consume the most energy without expensive sub-metering systems. This results in inefficient energy usage and higher costs without clear ways to optimize consumption.

Features Overview

Real-Time Energy Monitoring
Tracks 13 electrical metrics including voltage, current, power, frequency, and power factor.
Automated Circuit Protection
Detects 7 fault conditions and automatically trips the relay with safe restoration logic.
AI Energy Analytics
Generates twice-daily reports with cost estimates, efficiency ratings, and recommendations.
Appliance Identification (NILM)
Identifies active appliances using signature matching without additional sensors.
Web Dashboard Control
Remote relay control, live charts, and historical logs with 1-second refresh rate.
Offline + Online Operation
Fully functional locally with touchscreen UI even without internet connectivity.

How It Works

Data Collection

The ESP32 reads electrical data from the PZEM-004T sensor every 2 seconds and computes derived metrics such as cost, CO₂ output, and safety margin.

Protection Logic

The system evaluates 7 fault conditions (voltage, current, frequency, power factor, etc.). If thresholds are exceeded, the relay is automatically tripped and restored after a cooldown period.

Dual-Core Processing

One core handles real-time monitoring and protection, while the second core manages cloud communication and dashboard updates asynchronously.

Cloud & Dashboard

Data is sent to a PHP backend every 3 seconds. The web dashboard fetches updates every second to display live metrics and allow remote control.

AI & Intelligence Layer

Google Gemini processes aggregated data twice daily to generate energy reports, forecasts, and personalized recommendations.
