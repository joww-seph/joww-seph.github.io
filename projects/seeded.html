SeedEd

A portable, inflatable dome classroom powered by offline AI, 360-degree projection, and solar energy — designed to deliver immersive, adaptive education to remote communities without internet or infrastructure.

Problem

Access to quality education remains a global challenge, particularly in remote and underserved communities. Millions of children lack access to structured learning environments, while existing solutions often depend on internet connectivity, trained educators, and permanent infrastructure. This results in high illiteracy rates and limited foundational learning, especially in low-income regions. Traditional classroom models also fail to engage students effectively or adapt to individual learning needs.

Features Overview

Portable Dome Classroom
A 3m x 6m inflatable dome that creates a fully immersive, planetarium-like learning environment deployable anywhere.
Offline AI Learning Assistant
A fine-tuned LLaMa model that generates lessons, tracks progress, and adapts to each student without requiring internet access.
Solar-Powered System
Runs entirely on solar energy, enabling off-grid deployment in remote areas.
Structured Learning System
A 10-level literacy progression based on Bloom's Taxonomy and AVK methods for systematic skill development.

Tech Stack

AI & Data

Fine-tuned LLaMa (local inference), SQLite (offline data storage)

Interaction Systems

Speech-to-Text (input), Text-to-Speech (output), Interactive tablet interface

Infrastructure

360-degree projection system, Solar panel + battery system

Support System

Mobile app for educator monitoring and progress tracking

How It Works

Deployment

The dome is transported and inflated in a target rural or remote location.

Initialization

The system is configured for a 1–2 week learning session.

Learning Phase

Students interact with the offline AI, which personalizes lessons and tracks progress in real time.

Content Generation

The AI generates adaptive learning materials based on student performance.

Data Update Cycle

Once reconnected to the internet, collected data is used to refine and retrain the AI model.

Redeployment

The system is packed and transferred to the next community.

This workflow enables a continuous cycle of offline learning plus online improvement, ensuring scalability and long-term effectiveness.

Future Improvements

  • Integrate advanced AI models for deeper personalization and adaptive difficulty scaling
  • Expand curriculum beyond literacy into STEM and life skills education
  • Optimize hardware for faster deployment and reduced setup time
  • Develop cloud-sync capabilities for real-time monitoring when connectivity is available
  • Enhance user interface with more interactive and gamified learning experiences
