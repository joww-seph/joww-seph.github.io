Culture Shocked

An educational adventure game that transforms Philippine cultural heritage into an interactive, game-based learning experience. Explore a virtual museum, learn from NPC guides, and master traditional crafts through immersive simulations.

View on GitHub →

Problem

Cultural education in the Philippines is often delivered through static materials that fail to engage students or create lasting understanding. Many traditional crafts and indigenous practices are becoming less visible to younger generations due to limited exposure and modernization. Existing digital platforms rarely provide interactive, hands-on experiences that simulate real cultural processes.

Features Overview

Virtual Museum Exploration
Explore a curated space with 50+ interactive artifacts from diverse Filipino ethnic groups, each with contextual information and guided interaction.
NPC-Guided Learning (Lola System)
Grandmother NPCs provide cultural context, instructions, and narrative grounding through intergenerational storytelling.
Inabel Weaving Simulation
A step-by-step weaving system replicating real Ilocano textile processes including thread setup, heddle control, and shuttle movement.
Bilao (Basket) Weaving
A drag-and-drop bamboo weaving mechanic simulating pattern construction and material preparation.
Batok (Tattoo Art) System
A tapping-based tattoo simulation inspired by Kalinga traditions, incorporating rhythm, ink interaction, and pattern drawing.

Design Approach

How It Works

Exploration Phase

The player navigates a top-down virtual museum environment containing categorized cultural artifacts.

Interaction Layer

Each artifact triggers contextual information panels and NPC dialogue, introducing cultural background and significance.

Skill Engagement (Mini-Games)

Players enter interactive simulations (weaving, tattooing, crafting) where mechanics are designed to mirror real-world processes.

System Design Philosophy

Instead of abstract mini-games, each interaction follows procedural authenticity, meaning players learn actual steps involved in traditional crafts.

Feedback Loop

Progression is guided through NPC feedback, visual cues, and task completion, reinforcing both gameplay and learning.

Future Improvements

  • Expand artifact database to include more regional traditions and underrepresented groups
  • Introduce adaptive learning systems (AI-driven difficulty or guidance)
  • Add narrative progression and quest-based exploration
  • Implement audio elements (traditional music, voice lines) for deeper immersion
  • Optimize performance for mobile platforms and lower-end devices
